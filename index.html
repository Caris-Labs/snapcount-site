What you get

A whole season, kept from a folding chair.

Built by a coach who was tired of a clipboard and a spreadsheet that never got filled in. Everything in here exists because a real game needed it.

The game keeps itself

Pass, rush, flag pull, interception, sack, penalty, punt. Tap what happened, tap who did it. First downs, touchdowns, the point after, a pick six, halftime and the ends switching are all handled. Undo anything. Fix a play from ten plays ago and everything after it recalculates.

Your roster in a minute

Point the app at a screenshot of your league's roster and it reads the names and numbers straight off it, on your phone, without uploading anything. Or paste a list, or open a file. Tap a column to sort. It saves as you type.

Fall and spring, kept apart

Run more than one team and switch between them. Each team keeps its seasons separate, so a spring game never lands in your fall totals. Every filed game keeps its own box score, and the whole thing exports to a spreadsheet.

The best part

The grandparents can watch from four states away.

Turn on following and you get a six-character code to text round. Every family on your team can follow the score, the down, the plays and the box score as you keep them. It costs them nothing and they don't need an account.

Last names stay offWatchers see jersey numbers and first names. Full names only if you deliberately turn them on. These are children, and that is the default we chose.
The code belongs to one gameIt dies when you file the game, and you can stop following at any moment. Nobody can browse a list of who is playing tonight.
A bad signal is fineYou keep recording either way. The plays catch up when the sideline gets a bar back.
A parent can keep the bookHand the stats to someone in the stands while you coach. They don't pay anything either.
