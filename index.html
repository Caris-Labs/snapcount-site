SnapCount Flag Football

The book for your flag football team. Every play in a couple of taps. Score, downs and the field keep themselves. Stats for every player, all season.

SnapCount is made by Caris Labs for youth flag football coaches who keep their own stats from the sideline. Pass, rush, flag pull, interception: two taps and the scoreboard, the downs and the field are right. At the end of the season every player has a line.
