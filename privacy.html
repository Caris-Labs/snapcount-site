Privacy policy

SnapCount keeps a flag football team's stats. This page says what the app stores, where, and what happens to it. Last updated September 9, 2026.

What SnapCount stores

  • Your team's book: the team name, player numbers and names, the schedule, and every play logged during games. This lives on your phone.
  • If you sign in with Apple: your name and email address as Apple provides them (Apple lets you hide your email), used only to label your account. A copy of the team's book is then kept on our server so it is backed up and can be picked up on another phone.
  • If you choose to back up without an account: an anonymous account with no name or email, and the same server copy of the book.

What SnapCount does not do

  • No advertising, no ad networks, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No analytics or crash reporting services that receive your data.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with anyone.
  • Photos you choose for roster import are read on your phone by Apple's on-device text recognition and never leave it.

Where the server copy lives

Backups are stored with Supabase, a hosting provider, on servers in the United States, protected so that only your account can read your team's data.

Children

SnapCount is made for coaches and parents. Player rosters are entered by the coach and typically contain children's first and last names and jersey numbers. That information is used only to credit plays and is shown only to the account that entered it. We do not collect information from children directly.

Deleting your data

Settings → Account & backup → Delete my account removes your account and the server copy immediately. Deleting the app removes the copy on your phone. You can also email hello@carislabs.app and we will delete it for you.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
