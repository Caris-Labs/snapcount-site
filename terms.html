The Season Pass

What it is. An auto-renewing subscription, $19.99 a year in the United States, priced locally elsewhere. It lets you record games beyond the two free ones, for every team you run.

The free games. Your first two games are free and need no payment details.

Renewal. Payment is charged to your Apple account when you confirm the purchase. The subscription renews automatically each year unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the period ends.

Cancelling. Manage or cancel any time in your Apple account settings, under Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal; the current year runs to its end.

What stays yours

Everything you record belongs to you. Reading it, exporting it and backing it up stay free, with or without a Season Pass, and whether or not you ever subscribe again.

Using it fairly

Use SnapCount to keep your own teams' stats. Don't use it to collect other people's information without their permission, and don't try to get around the free-game limit or the Season Pass.

Live following

When you let parents follow a game, anyone with that game's code can see it while it's live. Share the code with people you trust. Last names stay hidden unless you turn them on.
